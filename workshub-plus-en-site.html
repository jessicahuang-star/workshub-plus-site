4 Entry Points & 4 Breakpoints

Messages are spread across chat apps and email, breaking the flow of information, wasting time on searches and letting key details slip through.

Without a unified process, paper and online forms get mixed up, progress is hard to track, and reviews and decisions slow to a crawl.

Breakpoint 3

Complex shift patterns and schedules make attendance hard to see in real time, adding friction to timekeeping and management.

Scattered Assets

Before You Get Started

LINE WORKS provides communication and collaboration. WorksHub+ builds on top of it with organization governance, a Bot Marketplace, system integration and audit capabilities.
A core goal of WorksHub+ is lowering the technical barrier to building and running Bots. HR, IT or department admins can manage the organization, members, permissions and Bot settings from the console, and use system integration and event triggers to turn data and events from ERP, CRM, HRM and other systems into LINE WORKS Bot notifications and workflows. The actual integration approach is planned around your existing systems and needs.
WorksHub+ helps you set up Bot notifications, keyword replies, rich menus, default messages, attendance check-in, internal Q&A, department notices and resource booking alerts, as well as event alerts and data notifications after connecting ERP, CRM, HRM and other systems.
The automations you can adopt depend on your existing systems, data sources and work scenarios.
